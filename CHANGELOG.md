# Changelog

What changed for you in each version of Mini Monitor, newest first.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-17

- **New** · Drives the whole family of small USB screens (2.1" round, 5", 8.8", not all tested yet) and recognises the one you plugged in.
- **New** · Portrait or landscape, upside down if your case insists, and the round screen gets a layout that fits inside the circle.
- **New** · Runs on Linux too, and with no screen plugged in, a window on the desktop shows what it would display.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-17

- **New** · Two rings show how much of your Claude session and week you've used, with your PC's vitals below. A grey question mark beats a stale number.
- **New** · Set it and forget it: it gets the screen back after sleep or an unplug, and renews its Claude sign-in before the rings go grey.
